How it works
• Set time to 07:00 to get fresh prices every morning before the team starts work
• The browser tab must remain open for the schedule to trigger
• Prices are saved to Firebase and visible to all 20 users instantly
• Schedule is saved locally — restores automatically when you reopen the app
• Uses Asia/Dubai timezone (UAE GMT+4) — no DST adjustments needed
